# Privacy Policy – Ball Passage Test

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

**Ball Passage Test does not collect, transmit or share any personal or usage data.**

- All calculations are performed locally on your device.
- The app does not request the internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
- The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries.
- Your ball set and up to 8 recent tube dimensions are stored only on your device. If Android
  backup is enabled on your device, this data is included in your device's own backup, which is
  controlled by you and your device settings. Clearing the app data or uninstalling the app
  deletes it.
