Ball Passage Test calculates the steel ball diameter for the ball passage test (ball test, Kugeldurchlaufversuch) of bent tubes and tube coils, as used in boiler and pressure-part manufacturing.

Enter the outer diameter, wall thickness and smallest bend radius of the tube. The minimum ball diameter and the allowed ball range are shown instantly, according to VGB-S-013-00-2014-12, section 7.2.5, Table 3.

• Instant result while you type, also on small phones: the result stays visible above the keyboard
• Your ball set: add the balls you have in the workshop and the app tells you which one to use
• Step-by-step calculation: inner diameter, bend ratio R/dₒ and the deduction from the table
• Table 3 with the cell used for your tube highlighted
• Clear guidance when a tube is outside the table, e.g. the minimum bend radius
• Recent tubes for one-tap recalculation
• Share the result as text for reports and messages
• Inner diameters of 50–57 mm are supported with extrapolated values and are always clearly marked as outside the standard
• Decimal comma and decimal point, English, Polish and German
• Light and dark theme, Material You colours
• Works fully offline, no ads, no tracking, no data collection

This app is an aid only. Always check results against the current edition of the standard and the requirements of your project.
